### qhashrope

A quantum verification prototype combining Hashrope and MultiSpecQR with fingerprint circuits, superdense coding, and SWAP-test experiments. Built for Florida Tech’s Qiskit Fall Fest, with simulation results and IBM Quantum hardware experiments.

[Code & experiments](https://github.com/jemsbhai/qhashrope) [Watch presentation](https://muntasersyed.com/speaking.html#recording-qhashrope) [Hashrope](https://muntasersyed.com/software.html#hashrope) [MultiSpecQR](https://muntasersyed.com/software.html#multispecqr)

### qfourierrope

Spectral phase fingerprints for quantum equality testing, with Qiskit simulations, noise studies, and record-deduplication experiments. Extends qhashrope's fingerprint research.

[Code & experiments](https://github.com/jemsbhai/qfourierrope) [Experiment studies](https://github.com/jemsbhai/qfourierrope/blob/main/EXPERIMENTS.md) [qhashrope](https://muntasersyed.com/projects.html#qhashrope) [Hashrope](https://muntasersyed.com/hashrope.html)
