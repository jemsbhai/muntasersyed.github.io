# About my work.

I’m a Lead Gen AI Engineer, AI systems researcher, open-source builder, and technical educator working across trust, efficiency, and agent infrastructure.

![Portrait of Muntaser Syed](https://images.ctfassets.net/kv878odoqyd1/7yhyBrSwM8N2uvDmMaczhM/fbefac391b0dafc29a60be6cb48baea9/muntaser-syed.jpg?h=700&w=560&fit=fill&f=face)Melbourne, Florida AI systems

Research focus

## AI systems should report their limits.

My current work covers confidence representation, provenance, temporal validity, context budgets, and edge deployment.

My doctoral work in computer engineering at Florida Institute of Technology examines artificial intelligence of things and effective machine learning on edge devices. Related projects cover semantic data, agent infrastructure, energy measurement, health sensing, compact data structures, and explainability.

Professionally, I work as a Lead Gen AI Engineer at Insight Global. I previously served as a technical lead at NVIDIA, and I contribute to DeepStation as an AI Advocate.

I also teach through conference sessions, workshops, and public repositories. These projects help me evaluate the methods in practice.
