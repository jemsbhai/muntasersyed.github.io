TEAM COLLABORATION · HARDWARE PROTOTYPE

Dewin / envlab

An October 2026 hardware-hackathon collaboration connecting a mobile exposure timeline with nearby air and water sensor readings, plus a check-in kiosk. The prototype uses hobby-grade sensors and shared demo access; its measurements do not establish health or drinking-water safety.

Repository & team components
HACKATHON PROTOTYPE · SEPTEMBER 12, 2026

Station Steward

A local agent connecting a real Arduino sensor bench and a simulated robotic workcell through Pollard, Chronofy, MCP-Edge, and human task handoffs. Built for the AI Tinkerers / OpenAI Agents Everywhere hackathon in Miami.

RepositoryAgent infrastructureTemporal stack
COLLABORATION · HACKATHON PROTOTYPE

Gatehouse

A vendor-review and onboarding prototype built with Katherine Burge. Muntaser contributed Pollard evidence ledgers, action/refusal records, seals, telemetry, and offline replay; Katherine built the agent fleets and cloud deployment.

Project & contributionsCollaborator-owned repositoryPollard
TEAM COLLABORATION · SHELLHACKS 2026

Grid Hazard / FieldSight

A team prototype combining infrastructure coordination with simulated rover, crawler, and arm inspections. Demonstrations use synthetic records and separately tested components; the complete workflow has not been validated as a live autonomous deployment.

EXPERIMENTAL PROTOTYPE · UNRELEASED

WattWarden

Energy-governed agents with provisioning tools, Pollard execution controls, and documented Arm cloud and phone experiments. Server energy is predicted by the TOML model; phone energy is measured through battery telemetry.

RepositoryExperimental logbookChallenge submissionPollard
COLLABORATOR-OWNED · CHALLENGE SUBMISSION

Joulehound

An Arm-device energy-profiling collaboration for local AI-agent workloads, comparing measured battery telemetry with FLOPs and NEXUS/TOML estimates. The collaborator-owned prototype was submitted to the Arm Create: AI Optimization Challenge; no award or stable release is claimed.
