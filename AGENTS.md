# Content preferences

- This is Muntaser Syed's personal portfolio. Write about his work directly,
  confidently, and in his voice. He is a full author of his research.
- Do not add editorial critiques, defensive caveats, or disclaimers about
  authorship, contribution, presentation delivery, or whether a source names
  him individually. Do not use distancing labels such as “program-listed
  contribution,” “virtual author contribution,” or “organizer-listed role.”
- Describe conference work as papers, talks, or presentations. Do not emphasize
  delivery formats such as pre-recorded or virtual as qualifications of the work.
  Include practical attendance information only when useful to the reader.
- Use “paper,” “presentation,” and “talk” consistently in public copy and
  metadata. Do not label work as posters, short papers, full papers, abstracts,
  or similar ranked categories. Describe the work and its substance directly.
- Remove language that downplays the work, including defensive status notes,
  evidence-boundary disclaimers, and editorial assessments of its significance.
- Keep source-checking commentary and uncertainty-management boilerplate out
  of public page copy and metadata. Omit unavailable details rather than adding
  disclaimers about them. Preserve accurate titles, author lists, dates, links,
  and publication status without inventing details.
- Apply these preferences across the entire website and future updates.
- Do not mention money, monetary amounts, or financial award or funding details
  in website content, maintained documentation, or user-facing responses unless
  the user explicitly asks for them.
- Describe awards through the result, project, team contribution, and supporting
  sources. A source containing financial details does not authorize repeating
  those details.
